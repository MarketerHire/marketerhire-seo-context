### Tagline

`: Your AI-Native Marketing Team`

---

## Logo Placement

### Positioning

The logo is typically placed as a **watermark** or **persistent brand mark**:

- **Corner placement** preferred (bottom-left or top-right depending on format)
- Vertically centered with accompanying tagline text
- Subtle presence — it should be visible but not dominant

### Sizing

| Context              | Logo Height | Notes                          |
| -------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1920x1080 canvas     | 70px        | Standard video/presentation    |
| 1080x1920 vertical   | 50px        | Social stories, reels          |
| Web header           | 40px        | Navigation bars                |
| Favicon / icon       | 32px or 16px| Browser tabs, app icons        |

Scale proportionally. The logo should never be smaller than 30px in height for legibility.

---

## Logo + Tagline Lockup

When displayed together:

```
[Logo Image]  MH1 : Your AI-Native Marketing Team
```

- Gap between logo and text: `18px`
- Tagline font: Inter, 24px, weight 600
- Tagline color: `#ffffff` with `letter-spacing: 0.5px`
- Vertical alignment: center

### Lockup Container

- Background: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)` (semi-transparent dark)
- Padding: `8px 16px`
- Border-radius: subtle rounding (4-8px)

---

## Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "M" character in the wordmark. No other text, icons, or decorative elements should intrude on this space.

---

## Logo Don'ts

- Don't stretch, skew, or rotate the logo
- Don't place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds without a backing container
- Don't recolor the logo — use the provided white version on dark backgrounds
- Don't use the logo smaller than 30px in height
- Don't add drop shadows, outlines, or effects to the logo itself
- Don't separate the colon from the tagline (`: Your AI-Native Marketing Team` is one unit)
