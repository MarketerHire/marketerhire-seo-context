## Section Guidelines

### Hook Intro
**Goal:** Immediately answer the query and hook the reader.

**Do:**
- Answer the main question in sentence 1-2
- Be specific (numbers, results, timeframes)
- Create urgency or relevance

**Don't:**
- Start with "In today's..."
- Provide background before the answer
- Use generic promises

**Example (Good):**
> AI marketing tools can automate 60-80% of repetitive marketing tasks. Here are the 10 that actually work, based on testing them across 50+ client accounts over the past year.

**Example (Bad):**
> In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, marketers are increasingly turning to artificial intelligence to streamline their workflows...

### Quick Answer Section
**Goal:** Win the Featured Snippet.

**Format for different snippet types:**
- **Definition:** "{{TERM}} is {{DEFINITION in 40-50 words}}."
- **List:** Immediately follow H2 with numbered list
- **Table:** Use markdown table with clear headers

### Core Sections
**Goal:** Comprehensive coverage of each subtopic.

**Each section should include:**
1. Definition/explanation of the concept
2. Why it matters (specific benefits)
3. How to do it (actionable steps)
4. Real examples or case studies
5. Common mistakes or pitfalls

**Word count:** 800-1,500 words per core section

### Implementation Section
**Goal:** Give readers a clear starting point.

**Include:**
- Numbered, sequential steps
- Specific tools or resources
- Time estimates
- Expected outcomes at each step

### FAQ Section
**Goal:** Capture long-tail queries and PAA questions.

**Requirements:**
- Use questions from PAA research
- Keep answers concise (50-100 words)
- Use schema-ready H3 format
- 5-10 questions total

### Conclusion
**Goal:** Reinforce key message and drive action.

**Include:**
- 1-2 sentence summary of main takeaway
- Specific CTA (not just "learn more")
- No new information

## Checklist Before Submission

```
Content Quality:
[ ] Title question answered in first 300 words
[ ] At least 3 specific examples or case studies
[ ] At least 5 statistics or data points cited
[ ] Personal experience or unique insight included
[ ] All PAA questions answered

SEO:
[ ] Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words
[ ] Secondary keywords in H2s
[ ] Meta description ready (150-160 chars)
[ ] FAQ section with 5+ questions
[ ] Internal links identified (4-8)
[ ] External links to authoritative sources (2-4)

Voice:
[ ] No AI words (delve, comprehensive, leverage, etc.)
[ ] No AI phrases (in today's world, it's important, etc.)
[ ] Sentence length varies
[ ] Personal opinions stated with reasoning
[ ] Limitations acknowledged where appropriate
```
