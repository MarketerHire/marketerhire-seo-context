## Usage Notes

1. **Headline-ready quotes** are strongest for pull quotes, article titles, social proof sections, and hero banners.
2. **Pain point quotes** work best in problem-agitation content and "before" sections of case studies.
3. **Negative reviews** should be used for objection-handling content — acknowledge the concern, then counter.
4. **Results quotes with specific metrics** are strongest for E-E-A-T credibility.
5. **Persona mapping** ensures each piece of content uses quotes that resonate with the target reader.
6. Verify all quotes against original sources before publication.
