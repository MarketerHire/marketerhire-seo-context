## Quick-Reference Voice Checklist

Before publishing any content, verify:

- [ ] Does the opening sentence contain a specific claim, scenario, or data point?
- [ ] Can any adjective be replaced with a number or concrete detail?
- [ ] Does each section contain at least one specific metric, example, or named reference?
- [ ] Are there zero AI-tell phrases from the Forbidden list?
- [ ] Does the piece speak to one or more of your defined personas?
- [ ] Would your target reader feel this was written by someone who understands their job?
- [ ] Is there a clear CTA or next step?
