# MarketerHire — Audience Personas

## Persona 1: Sarah — The Scaling VP Marketing

**Role:** VP of Marketing at a Series B SaaS company (50-150 employees, $10-30M revenue)
**Archetype:** The Stretched Leader

**Daily Reality:** Manages a lean team of 3-5 marketers, reports to CEO, owns pipeline targets. Constantly triaging between strategy and execution. Has budget for specialists but can't justify full-time hires for every channel.

**Core Fears:**
- Missing pipeline targets because she can't staff fast enough
- Hiring a full-time specialist who turns out to be wrong for the role ($150K+ mistake)
- Falling behind competitors who are investing in channels she can't cover

**Deep Motivations:**
- Build a high-performing marketing org without bloating headcount
- Prove marketing's revenue impact to the board
- Get specialist execution off her plate so she can focus on strategy

**Trust Builders:**
- Seeing the marketer's actual portfolio and past results
- Fast time-to-value (results in weeks, not months)
- Flexibility to scale up/down as priorities shift

**Trust Killers:**
- Sales pressure before understanding her needs
- Junior people assigned after signing
- Long-term contracts with no exit

**Critical Pain Points:**
- Headcount freeze but pipeline targets increasing
- Can't find senior specialists willing to work fractional
- Agency burned her before — junior staff, no accountability

## Persona 2: Alex — The First-Time Founder

**Role:** CEO/Founder at a seed-to-Series A startup (5-20 employees, $1-5M revenue)
**Archetype:** The Builder Who Doesn't Know Marketing

**Daily Reality:** Wearing every hat. Built the product, closed early customers, but marketing is a black box. Knows they need marketing but doesn't know what "good" looks like or how to evaluate talent.

**Core Fears:**
- Wasting $50-100K on the wrong marketing hire or agency
- Getting sold tactics they don't need
- Not knowing enough to manage a marketer effectively

**Deep Motivations:**
- Get to product-market fit faster through better distribution
- Stop being the sole source of leads
- Build a repeatable growth engine

**Trust Builders:**
- Plain-language explanation of what they need (not jargon)
- Quick wins that prove the approach works
- Hearing from founders at similar stage who succeeded

**Trust Killers:**
- Upselling before delivering results
- Complex proposals with unclear ROI
- "Trust the process" without transparency

**Critical Pain Points:**
- "I know I don't know how to hire the right person" (direct quote from discovery calls)
- Tried Upwork, got burned
- Can't afford a bad hire at this stage

## Persona 3: Marcus — The Burned Founder

**Role:** CEO at a growth-stage company (20-50 employees, $5-15M revenue)
**Archetype:** The Post-Agency Refugee

**Daily Reality:** Has tried 2-3 agencies and/or freelancers. Spent $100K+ on marketing that didn't work. Skeptical of promises, demanding of accountability. Wants results, not decks.

**Core Fears:**
- Getting burned again by marketers who overpromise and underdeliver
- Wasting another quarter on the wrong approach
- Not being able to tell the difference between good and bad marketing

**Deep Motivations:**
- Find one trustworthy marketing partner, not a revolving door
- See measurable ROI tied to revenue, not vanity metrics
- Stop managing marketing and get back to running the business

**Trust Builders:**
- Concrete metrics and case studies from similar companies
- Trial period to validate before committing
- Direct access to the person doing the work (not an account manager)

**Trust Killers:**
- "We're one of many clients" (direct customer quote)
- Vague reporting and excuses
- Being handed off to junior staff

**Critical Pain Points:**
- "I've been through multiple different marketing agencies" (direct customer quote)
- "Agencies often assign more junior people to small accounts" (direct customer quote)
- "Everyone says they can do everything" — can't differentiate vendors

## Persona 4: David — The CMO Under Pressure

**Role:** CMO or Head of Growth at a Series B-C company (100-300 employees, $20-50M revenue)
**Archetype:** The Efficiency Optimizer

**Daily Reality:** Owns a $2-5M marketing budget. Has a team of 8-15 but gaps in specialized channels. Board wants efficiency — more pipeline per dollar. Hiring full-time takes too long; agencies are too expensive for what they deliver.

**Core Fears:**
- Board questioning marketing spend efficiency
- Losing market share while waiting to hire
- Team burnout from covering too many channels

**Deep Motivations:**
- Fill specialist gaps without adding headcount
- Prove marketing efficiency to the board
- Build a scalable, elastic marketing capability

**Trust Builders:**
- Vetted senior talent (not juniors learning on his budget)
- Speed — needs someone productive in days, not weeks
- Flexibility to adjust scope as strategy evolves

**Trust Killers:**
- Cookie-cutter approaches that don't account for his market
- Slow onboarding or ramp time
- Having to manage the marketplace itself (wants it handled)

**Critical Pain Points:**
- Headcount freeze but targets not adjusted
- Can't find fractional CMO-caliber talent on their own
- Team stretched across too many channels — quality dropping
