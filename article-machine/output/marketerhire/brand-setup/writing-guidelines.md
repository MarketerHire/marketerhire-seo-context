# MarketerHire — Writing Guidelines

## Brand Voice

**Five dimensions:**

| Dimension | Level | Description |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| **Formality** | Medium-low | Startup-energetic, professional but not corporate. Write like a smart colleague, not a consultant. |
| **Expertise** | High | We know marketing hiring inside and out. 30,000+ matches worth of pattern recognition. State things confidently. |
| **Confidence** | High (not arrogant) | Declarative sentences for facts. No hedging with "might" or "could potentially." |
| **Warmth** | Medium | Empathetic to the reader's pain, but not sappy. Acknowledge their frustration, then resolve it. |
| **Pacing** | Fast | Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. Get to the point. Founders are busy. |

## Tone by Content Type

| Type | Tone shift |
|------|-----------|
| **Blog / How-to** | Practical, direct, data-backed. Lead with the answer, then explain. |
| **Comparison / Listicle** | Fair but opinionated. Acknowledge strengths of alternatives, then differentiate clearly. |
| **Pillar guide** | Authoritative, comprehensive. The definitive resource. Cite data liberally. |
| **Thought leadership** | Provocative, point-of-view driven. Challenge conventional wisdom. |

## Sentence and Paragraph Style

- **Sentence length:** 12-20 words average. Mix short punchy sentences (5-8 words) with medium explanatory ones. Never exceed 30 words.
- **Paragraph length:** 1-3 sentences. 4 sentences max. White space is your friend.
- **Reading level:** Grade 8-10. Smart but accessible. No academic jargon.
- **Structure:** Front-load the point. Answer before explain. Conclusion before evidence.

## Forbidden Words and Phrases

**Corporate jargon (never use):**
- synergy, leverage (as verb), holistic, solution, ecosystem, paradigm
- "best-in-class," "world-class," "cutting-edge," "state-of-the-art"
- "unlock potential," "unlock growth," "level up," "dream team"

**AI-tell phrases (never use):**
- "In today's fast-paced..." / "In the ever-evolving..."
- "It's important to note that..."
- "Here's the thing" / "Here's why"
- "Let's dive in" / "Let's explore" / "Let's unpack"
- "At the end of the day"
- "Navigate the complexities"
- "It's not X, it's Y" (lazy contrast)
- Rhetorical questions as hooks
- Starting paragraphs with "So," or "Now,"
- "Comprehensive" / "robust" / "seamless" / "streamlined"

**Structural crutches (avoid):**
- Lists of three as a rhythm crutch ("X, Y, and Z" repeated)
- Superlatives without proof
- "According to experts" (name the expert or cut it)

## Style Markers — What Makes MarketerHire Voice Distinctive

1. **Lead with outcomes, not features.** "Matched in 48 hours" not "Our platform uses AI matching."
2. **Use concrete numbers.** 30,000+ matches. 95% trial-to-hire. 48 hours. <5% acceptance. Always cite the proof.
3. **Empathy before pitch.** Acknowledge pain, then resolve it. Never pitch before earning trust.
4. **One idea per message.** If you can't say it in one sentence, simplify.
5. **Real customer language.** Use phrases from actual discovery calls and testimonials, not marketing-speak.

## Good/Bad Examples

### Example 1: Opening a blog post

**Bad:**
> In today's ever-evolving marketing landscape, businesses of all sizes face the challenge of finding the right marketing talent. Whether you're a startup or an enterprise, building a world-class marketing team is crucial for unlocking growth.

**Good:**
> You need a senior growth marketer. Your board wants results by Q3. Full-time hiring takes 3-6 months. Agencies will assign a junior. Here's your third option.

### Example 2: Describing MarketerHire

**Bad:**
> MarketerHire is a comprehensive, best-in-class platform that leverages cutting-edge technology to provide holistic marketing solutions for businesses seeking to level up their marketing efforts.

**Good:**
> MarketerHire matches you with a vetted marketing expert in 48 hours. Month-to-month. No long-term contracts. 95% of trials convert to ongoing engagements — because when the match is right, you know fast.

### Example 3: Addressing competition

**Bad:**
> Unlike other solutions in the market, MarketerHire offers a truly unique and innovative approach that sets us apart from the competition.

**Good:**
> Agencies spread your budget across junior staff. Upwork gives you a resume and a prayer. Full-time hiring takes a quarter and $150K. MarketerHire gives you a vetted specialist, working, in 48 hours.

## Proof Stack (Use Liberally)

When making claims, back them with these verified data points:
- 30,000+ successful matches
- 95% trial-to-hire rate
- 48 hours to first match
- 6,000+ customers
- Top 5% vetted marketers / <5% acceptance rate
- Trust logos: Netflix, Plaid, Tinuiti, Constant Contact, MasterClass
