# MarketerHire — Company Profile

## The Problem

Growing companies face a broken hiring market for marketing talent:

- **Agencies disappoint.** Junior staff on your account, one of many clients, long contracts, opaque results. 46% of prospects have tried an agency before coming to MarketerHire.
- **Full-time hiring is slow and risky.** 3-6 months to hire, $100K+ commitment, no guarantee of fit. 37% of prospects are evaluating a full-time hire.
- **DIY and Upwork burn time.** Unvetted freelancers, no quality guarantee, management overhead. 12% of prospects are juggling unvetted freelancers.
- **The skills gap is real.** Many companies — especially post-acquisition or early-stage — have zero marketing infrastructure and don't know how to evaluate marketing talent.

## What We Do

MarketerHire is a talent marketplace and AI growth platform that matches companies with expert fractional marketers in 48 hours.

**Two products:**

1. **MarketerHire Core** — Expert fractional marketers matched in 48 hours. Vetted top 5%, month-to-month, 2-week trial. $7-10K/mo typical.
2. **MH-1** — AI Growth Team. Full-stack growth capability combining expert marketers with AI-powered execution. Deployed in days, not months. $10-30K/mo.

## Who This Is For

**Ideal customers:**
- Startup founders (Series A-C), 10-50 employees, $2-20M revenue
- VP/Director of Marketing at companies with 10-200 employees
- CMOs and Heads of Growth with stretched teams or headcount freezes
- PE-backed companies scaling post-acquisition

**Best industries:** B2B SaaS/Tech, E-commerce/DTC, Agencies, Professional Services

**Not a fit:** Companies seeking the cheapest option, those needing full-time permanent employees, enterprise companies with 500+ employees and mature in-house teams.

## How It Works

1. **Tell us what you need** — Role, skills, budget, timeline
