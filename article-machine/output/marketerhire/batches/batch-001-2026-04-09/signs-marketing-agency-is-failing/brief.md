## Section 2: Competitive Intelligence

### Competitor 1: SEO Werkz — "7 Signs Your Digital Marketing Agency Isn't Working"
- Structure: 7-item listicle, short descriptions per sign
- Word count: ~1,200
- Strengths: Clean format, scannable
- Gaps: No metric benchmarks, no next-steps framework, no scorecard, no real examples

### Competitor 2: Laire Digital — "7 Signs Your Current Marketing Agency Is Not a Good Fit"
- Structure: 7-item listicle with paragraph explanations
- Word count: ~1,400
- Strengths: Addresses fit vs. failure distinction
- Gaps: No data, no self-assessment tool, no alternative models discussed, no customer voice

### Competitor 3: PathOpt — "7 Signs Your Marketing Agency Is Failing"
- Structure: 7-item numbered list
- Word count: ~1,000
- Strengths: Direct headline match
- Gaps: Extremely thin content, no benchmarks, no action plan, no transition guidance

### AI Overview Analysis
- Currently triggered: Likely (listicle format with clear question)
- Sources cited: Mix of small agency blogs — no dominant authority
- Content format: Bulleted list of warning signs
- Gap: No source provides metric benchmarks, self-assessment tools, or transition frameworks. Opportunity to become the AI-cited authority by adding structured data and actionable next steps.

### Content Differentiation Strategy
Our article beats all 3 competitors by:
1. **More signs (11 vs. 7)** — more coverage signals authority
2. **Metric benchmarks for each sign** — specific numbers, not vague warnings
3. **Self-assessment scorecard** — interactive diagnostic tool (unique to our article)
4. **Transition framework** — what to do AFTER identifying the problem
5. **Real customer quotes** — from MarketerHire discovery calls (agency frustration quotes)
6. **Alternative model comparison** — fractional vs. agency vs. in-house (natural MH positioning)

## Full Outline

### INTRO (200-250 words)
- Open with: "46% of companies that come to MarketerHire have already tried an agency." Set the stakes — this is a pattern, not an isolated problem.
- Keywords to include: signs your marketing agency is failing, marketing agency red flags
- AEO requirement: first 100 words must directly list the top warning signs so AI can extract them as a snippet

### H2: Your CAC Is Climbing Without Explanation (250-300 words)
- Requirement: Define the specific metric benchmark — CAC increasing 20%+ over 3 months without clear external factors. Explain what a healthy CAC trend looks like vs. agency neglect.
- Keywords: primary — marketing agency red flags, secondary — how to know if your marketing agency is bad
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block defining the CAC red flag
- Format: paragraphs with a comparison benchmark callout

### H2: You Haven't Talked to a Senior Strategist in Weeks (200-250 words)
- Requirement: The bait-and-switch pattern. Define the benchmark: 6+ weeks without senior strategic input = deprioritized. Include customer quote about junior staff on accounts.
- Keywords: primary — signs your marketing agency is failing, secondary — agency bait-and-switch
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: paragraphs

### H2: Reports Are Full of Vanity Metrics (250-300 words)
- Requirement: Define vanity vs. revenue metrics. Provide the litmus test: can you trace agency reports to pipeline or revenue? If not, they're hiding behind volume.
- Keywords: primary — marketing agency red flags, secondary — agency reporting problems
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: table comparing vanity metrics vs. revenue metrics

### H2: They Can't Tell You What's Working and What Isn't (200-250 words)
- Requirement: The "pop quiz" test — ask your agency which campaign drove the most revenue last quarter. Hesitation or vague answers reveal a lack of analytical rigor.
- Keywords: primary — how to know if your marketing agency is bad, secondary — marketing agency accountability
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: paragraphs with a specific test question

### H2: Your Strategy Hasn't Changed in 6+ Months (200-250 words)
- Requirement: Static strategy in a dynamic market = autopilot. Algorithms update quarterly, competitors shift, customer behavior changes. A strong agency adapts. Define the 6-month rule.
- Keywords: primary — when to fire your marketing agency, secondary — marketing agency autopilot
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: paragraphs

### H2: You're Managing the Agency More Than They're Managing Your Marketing (200-250 words)
- Requirement: The role reversal test. If you're writing briefs, chasing deliverables, and reminding about deadlines, the value proposition has collapsed. Include customer quote about vendor management burden.
- Keywords: primary — what to do when marketing agency isn't working, secondary — agency management burden
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: paragraphs with bullet list of management tasks you shouldn't be doing

### H2: Their Team Has Turned Over and You Weren't Told (200-250 words)
- Requirement: Undisclosed staff changes, loss of context, quality dips. Benchmark: if 2+ people on your account changed in a quarter without proactive communication, they're understaffed.
- Keywords: primary — marketing agency red flags, secondary — agency staff turnover
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: paragraphs

### H2: You're Locked Into a Long Contract With No Performance Clauses (200-250 words)
- Requirement: 12-month minimums, auto-renewals, no performance exit ramps. Compare this to month-to-month alternatives. If the agency needs a contract to retain you instead of results, the incentives are misaligned.
- Keywords: primary — when to fire your marketing agency, secondary — marketing agency contracts
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: paragraphs

### H2: They Don't Know Your Customer (200-250 words)
- Requirement: The ICP quiz. Ask your agency to describe your ideal customer. If they give a generic answer or repeat stale information, they haven't invested in learning your business. Include customer quote about "everyone says they can do everything."
- Keywords: primary — how to know if your marketing agency is bad, secondary — agency ICP knowledge
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: paragraphs with quiz questions

### H2: Results Flatlined After the First 90 Days (250-300 words)
- Requirement: Initial improvements from low-hanging fruit (quick keyword fixes, obvious ad waste cuts), then stagnation. A strong agency has a roadmap beyond Month 3. Benchmark: if growth rate drops below 5% month-over-month after 90 days with no escalation plan, they've run out of ideas.
- Keywords: primary — how long to give marketing agency before results, secondary — marketing agency results timeline
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: paragraphs with timeline expectations

### H2: You Feel Like One of Many (200-250 words)
- Requirement: The emotional gut-check. They miss context in meetings. Generic recommendations. Account manager doesn't remember details. Include customer quotes: "We're one of many clients" and "I've been through multiple different marketing agencies."
- Keywords: primary — signs your marketing agency is failing, secondary — agency client prioritization
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: paragraphs

### H2: Agency Self-Assessment Scorecard (250-300 words)
- Requirement: Structured scoring tool. List all 11 signs as checkboxes. Score interpretation: 0-3 (healthy), 4-6 (warning zone), 7-10 (exit time). Actionable next step for each range.
- Keywords: primary — marketing agency red flags, secondary — when to fire your marketing agency
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: numbered checklist + score interpretation table

### H2: What to Do Next — Your Exit Plan (300-350 words)
- Requirement: Three-part framework: (1) How to have the conversation with your agency — specific talking points. (2) Transition checklist — data access, logins, assets, contract review. (3) Alternative models — fractional marketers, talent marketplaces, bringing it in-house. Natural MarketerHire integration: 48-hour matching, month-to-month, vetted top 5%.
- Keywords: primary — what to do when marketing agency isn't working, secondary — fractional marketer vs agency
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block
- Format: numbered process list + comparison table (agency vs. fractional vs. in-house)

  1. How do you know if your marketing agency is bad?
