## Full Outline

### INTRO (150-200 words)
- Open with the real cost of a wrong marketing hire — $50-100K wasted, plus months of lost growth
- Direct statement: a digital marketing consultant is a vetted specialist who builds and executes your growth strategy on a fractional basis
- Keywords to include: hire digital marketing consultant, digital marketing consultant
- AEO requirement: first 100 words must define what hiring a digital marketing consultant involves and why it matters — extractable as standalone answer

### H2: What Does a Digital Marketing Consultant Do? (300-350 words)
- Requirement: Define the role. List core responsibilities (strategy development, channel management, analytics and reporting, campaign execution, vendor oversight). Contrast with a marketing manager (strategic + execution) and an agency (dedicated vs. shared attention).
- Keywords: primary — what does a digital marketing consultant do, secondary — digital marketing consultant
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block defining the role
- Format: short paragraphs + bullet list of responsibilities

### H2: Signs You Need to Hire a Digital Marketing Consultant (250-300 words)
- Requirement: Business triggers that signal it's time — stalled growth, zero in-house marketing expertise, agency burnout, entering a new market or channel, headcount freeze with growing targets. Integrate customer voice quotes from discovery calls.
- Keywords: primary — hire digital marketing consultant, secondary — freelance digital marketing consultant
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block listing top 3 signals
- Format: bullet list of signals with brief explanations + customer quotes

### H2: How to Hire a Digital Marketing Consultant (Step by Step) (500-600 words)
- Requirement: Practical 5-step process: (1) Define your scope and goals, (2) Choose your hiring model (freelance, marketplace, referral), (3) Evaluate candidates on results not resumes, (4) Run a paid trial before committing, (5) Set KPIs and review cadence. Each step gets 80-120 words with specific advice.
- Keywords: primary — hire digital marketing consultant, secondary — digital marketing consultant for small business
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block summarizing the 5-step process
- Format: numbered list (HowTo schema compatible)

### H2: How Much Does a Digital Marketing Consultant Cost? (350-400 words)
- Requirement: Real pricing data — hourly ($100-300/hr), monthly retainer ($3,000-10,000/mo), project-based ($5,000-25,000). Comparison table: consultant vs. agency vs. full-time hire across cost, commitment, expertise level, flexibility, time to start.
- Keywords: primary — how much does a digital marketing consultant cost, secondary — digital marketing consultant vs agency
- AEO requirement: open with specific price range in first sentence
- Format: pricing paragraphs + comparison table

### H2: Digital Marketing Consultant vs. Agency vs. Full-Time Hire (300-350 words)
- Requirement: Head-to-head comparison across 6 dimensions: cost, time to start, expertise level, flexibility, accountability, best for. When each model is the right choice. Clear opinion on when a consultant wins.
- Keywords: primary — digital marketing consultant vs agency, secondary — freelance digital marketing consultant
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block stating the key difference
- Format: comparison table + short explanatory paragraphs

### H2: Where to Find Vetted Digital Marketing Consultants (300-350 words)
- Requirement: Source options ranked by quality: (1) Vetted talent marketplaces like MarketerHire, (2) Professional referrals, (3) LinkedIn search, (4) General freelance platforms. - Keywords: primary — freelance digital marketing consultant, secondary — hire digital marketing consultant
- AEO requirement: open with 40-60 word answer block on where to find consultants
- Format: ranked list with pros/cons for each source
