## Section 2: Competitive Intelligence

Competitive intelligence based on known competitive landscape:

### Competitor Landscape
- **Toptal** — Generalist marketplace. Covers fractional CMO but lacks marketing-specific vetting depth. Positions on broad talent pool.
- **Mayple** — Managed marketplace with AI matching. Offers packaged CMO services. Differentiates on data-driven matching.
- **Hawke Media** — Full-service agency model. "Outsourced CMO" positioning. Higher price point, less flexibility.
- **Right Side Up** — Hybrid agency/talent platform. Strong in growth/performance marketing fractional roles. Narrower specialization.
- **Upwork** — Commodity marketplace. Low prices, no vetting, high variance. Risk of junior talent.

### Content Gap Opportunities
- Most competitor content focuses on "what is a fractional CMO" without actionable hiring criteria
- Pricing transparency is weak across the board — opportunity to lead with real numbers
- Few articles address the comparison between fractional CMO, agency, and full-time hire in a structured table format
- PE/post-acquisition use case is underserved in existing content
- Trial period / risk mitigation angle is missing from most competitor articles
