## Section 2: Competitive Intelligence

Competitive intelligence built from context document and brand knowledge.

### Competitor Landscape (Pricing Content)

Most existing content on marketing consultant costs is generic, listing broad ranges without breaking down the variables that move the number. Common weaknesses:

- **Thin comparison tables** — list agencies, freelancers, and FTEs but skip fractional and marketplace models entirely
- **No real proof points** — vague claims like "agencies charge $5K-$25K/mo" with no source or context
- **Missing the "hidden cost" angle** — ignore onboarding time, management overhead, bad-hire risk, tool costs
- **No decision framework** — readers get ranges but no guidance on which model fits their situation

### Our Opportunity

- Include fractional/marketplace model as a distinct category (competitors skip this)
- Lead with MarketerHire's 30,000+ match dataset as a credible source for pricing ranges
- Break down hidden costs (bad-hire risk, onboarding lag, management time)
- Add a decision framework: "If your situation is X, model Y fits best"
