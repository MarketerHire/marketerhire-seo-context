## What Does a Freelance Digital Marketer Do?

A freelance digital marketer executes marketing strategy and campaigns across one or more digital channels — paid media, SEO, email, content marketing, or analytics — without requiring a full-time hire. They typically specialize in one or two channels and bring 5-10 years of experience to your business on a fractional basis.

The term "digital marketer" is broad, and that breadth is part of the problem. Everyone claims they can do everything. One discovery call from a MarketerHire customer put it bluntly: "One thing I've found in the marketing stuff is it seems everybody says they can do everything."

The best freelance digital marketers specialize. Here are the most common specialties and what they deliver:

| Specialty | What They Do | Typical Deliverables |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|
| Paid Search (PPC) | Manage Google Ads, Bing Ads campaigns | Campaign builds, bid strategies, ROAS reporting |
| Paid Social | Run Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok ads | Creative briefs, audience targeting, A/B tests |
| SEO | Improve organic rankings and traffic | Technical audits, keyword strategy, content plans |
| Content Marketing | Create blog posts, whitepapers, case studies | Editorial calendars, written content, distribution plans |

The key difference from an agency: a freelance digital marketer works on your business directly. No account manager middleman. No junior staff assigned to your account after you signed based on a senior person's pitch.

## How to Hire a Freelance Digital Marketer (Step by Step)

Hiring a freelance digital marketer takes five steps: define your scope, source candidates, vet for channel expertise, run a paid trial, and set measurable KPIs. Done right, you can go from search to working engagement in under a week. Done wrong, you'll cycle through 3-4 freelancers over six months.

### Step 1: Define the Scope and Channel

Write down exactly what you need before you start looking. Vague briefs attract generalists.

Be specific: "I need someone to manage $15K/month in Google Ads spend, optimize for lead quality not volume, and report weekly on ROAS and cost-per-SQL." That brief will filter out 80% of unqualified candidates immediately.

Decide whether you need a specialist (one channel, deep expertise) or a generalist (multiple channels, strategic oversight). Most growing companies need specialists first and a strategist later.

### Step 2: Source Candidates from the Right Places

Where you look determines what you find. [Freelancer websites](https://marketerhire.com/blog/best-freelancer-websites) range from unvetted marketplaces to curated talent networks. Upwork has volume but no quality filter. Referrals work but take time. Pre-vetted marketplaces like MarketerHire screen for channel expertise and match you in 48 hours — with a less than 5% acceptance rate for marketers who apply.

### Step 3: Vet for Channel Results, Not Resumes

Resumes tell you where someone worked. They don't tell you what they produced.

Ask for:
- Specific metrics from past engagements (ROAS, CPA, organic traffic growth, email conversion rates)
- Examples of strategy documents or campaign structures they built
- References from clients in your industry or at your stage
- Their process for the first 30 days on a new account

Red flag: a freelancer who can't name specific numbers from their last three engagements. Strong performers track their results.

### Step 4: Run a Paid Trial (2 Weeks Minimum)

Never commit to a long-term engagement without a trial. Two weeks is enough to evaluate work quality, communication habits, and strategic thinking. MarketerHire builds this into every engagement — and 95% of trials convert to ongoing relationships because the matching process front-loads the vetting.

During the trial, look for:
- Proactive communication (do they surface problems or wait for you to find them?)
- Deliverable quality in the first week
- Ability to explain their strategy in plain language

### Step 5: Set KPIs and a Review Cadence

Define success metrics before work starts. Not vanity metrics like impressions. Revenue-connected KPIs: cost per SQL, ROAS, pipeline sourced from organic, email-attributed revenue. One MarketerHire customer defined it clearly: "Success would look like when we go on our scorecard metrics, that we're hitting all the numbers."

Set a weekly check-in for the first month. Then move to biweekly. Monthly reporting with a quarterly strategy review is the steady-state for most freelance engagements.

## How Much Does a Freelance Digital Marketer Cost?

Most freelance digital marketers charge between $75 and $200 per hour, or $5,000 to $15,000 per month on a retainer basis. Rates vary by specialty, seniority, and whether you hire directly or through a vetted marketplace. Senior specialists with 8+ years of experience in high-demand channels (paid search, growth marketing) command the top of that range.

Here's what to expect by channel and seniority level:

| Specialty | Mid-Level (3-5 yrs) | Senior (5-8 yrs) | Expert (8+ yrs) |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Paid Search / PPC | $75-$120/hr | $120-$175/hr | $175-$225/hr |
| Paid Social | $70-$110/hr | $110-$160/hr | $160-$200/hr |
| SEO | $80-$125/hr | $125-$175/hr | $175-$250/hr |
| Content Marketing | $60-$100/hr | $100-$150/hr | $150-$200/hr |
| Email / Lifecycle | $70-$115/hr | $115-$165/hr | $165-$210/hr |
| Growth / Performance | $90-$140/hr | $140-$190/hr | $190-$250/hr |

These rates reflect the U.S. market for experienced professionals. Rates on unvetted platforms like Upwork skew lower ($25-$75/hr) but so does the average quality.

Three factors drive pricing more than anything:

1. **Channel demand.** Paid search and growth marketing freelancers cost more because ROI is directly measurable and demand outpaces supply.
2. **Seniority and track record.** A freelancer who's managed $1M+/month in ad spend commands more than someone with $10K/month experience. Results are the currency.
3. **Engagement model.** Monthly retainers typically cost less per hour than project-based work because the freelancer has income predictability. Through a marketplace like MarketerHire, expect $7,000-$10,000/month for a dedicated expert — with the vetting already done.
