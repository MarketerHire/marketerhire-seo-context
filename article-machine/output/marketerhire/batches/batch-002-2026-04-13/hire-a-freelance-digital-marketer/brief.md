## Section 2: Competitive Intelligence

Competitive intelligence built from context document and market knowledge.

### Competitor Landscape (Direct Hiring Platforms)

- **Upwork** — Broad freelancer marketplace. High volume, low vetting. Searchers who've used Upwork often arrive at MarketerHire after quality disappointments.
- **Toptal** — Positions as "top 3%" talent. Generalist marketplace, not marketing-specialized. Higher price, slower matching.
- **Mayple** — AI-matched marketing talent. Managed marketplace with packaged services. Smaller network than MarketerHire.
- **Right Side Up** — Hybrid agency/talent model. Fractional marketers, but more agency-like engagement model.
- **Hawke Media** — Full-service agency positioning as outsourced CMO. Not a freelancer marketplace.

### Content Gap Opportunities

- Most competitor content is generic "how to hire a freelancer" — not specific to digital marketing specialties
- Few articles address the vetting problem with specific acceptance rate data
- Comparison content between freelancer, agency, and full-time is surface-level — opportunity to add real cost data
- No competitor content uses 30,000+ match dataset insights

### AI Overview Analysis

- Likely triggered for this query given transactional intent
- Expected format: numbered steps or brief paragraph
- Gap: AI Overviews won't have proprietary data (match rates, trial-to-hire stats)
