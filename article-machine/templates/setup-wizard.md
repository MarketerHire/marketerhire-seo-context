# Setup Wizard — Intake Template

Present this to the user at the start of Phase 1.

---

```
Welcome to the Article Machine. Let's set up your content intelligence system.

I need a few details to get started. Answer as many as you can — I'll research the rest.

1. **Company name**
   What's the brand? (This becomes the folder name — lowercase, hyphenated)

2. **Website**
   What's the company URL?

3. **Industry**
   What space are you in? (e.g., "B2B SaaS", "ecommerce", "marketing services")

4. **Target audience**
   Who are you trying to reach? Be specific:
   - Roles (CMO, marketing manager, founder)
   - Company size (startup, mid-market, enterprise)
   - What they're looking for

5. **Competitors** (2-5)
   Name your main competitors. Include websites if you know them.

6. **Content goal**
   What's the primary goal of this content?
   □ Organic traffic
   □ Lead generation
   □ Thought leadership
   □ Product signups
   □ Brand awareness

7. **Author**
   Who should be the byline?
   - Name:
   - Title:
   - One-line bio:

8. **Existing blog**
   Does the company have a blog? What's the URL pattern?
   (e.g., example.com/blog/, example.com/resources/)
```

---

After collecting answers, confirm:

```
Here's what I have:

Company: {name} ({url})
Industry: {industry}
Audience: {audience_summary}
Competitors: {list}
Goal: {goal}
Author: {name}, {title}
Blog: {blog_url}

I'll now build your brand intelligence system. This takes about 15-20 minutes and produces:
• Company profile
• 3-5 buyer personas
• 50-100 question bank
• Writing guidelines
• Customer voice library
• Content inventory

Proceed? [y/n]
```
